Why Sharing Matters

JAMES AI is a fully open-source, 100% private, browser-based AI terminal. It runs completely locally on your device using WebGPU—which means zero tracking, zero accounts, and zero cloud servers.

Unlike massive corporate AI products backed by billions of dollars in VC funding, JAMES is built by a single developer (Andrey Lopukhov). There is no marketing budget, no ads, and no premium subscriptions.

The only way a project like this survives and grows is through grassroots support. If you believe in privacy-first AI, offline capability, and open-source tools, please take a second to share JAMES with your network, your coworkers, or your friends.
